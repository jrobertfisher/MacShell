NAME
    chattr - set the locked and invisible flags

SYNOPSIS
    chattr [+-][li] <file>

DESCRIPTION
    Turns flags on (+) or off (-): l is locked, i is invisible (hidden in
    the Finder; ls -a still shows it).

EXAMPLES
    chattr +i secret.txt
    chattr -l notes.txt

SEE ALSO
    chmod, ls, getinfo
