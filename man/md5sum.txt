NAME
    md5sum - MD5 checksum of a file

SYNOPSIS
    md5sum <file>

DESCRIPTION
    Prints the MD5 checksum of a file's data fork, in the same format as
    md5sum on Linux, to check a download against a published value.
    MD5 and SHA-1 are fine for spotting damage but not for security;
    use sha256sum for that.

SEE ALSO
    sha256sum, crc32, cmp
